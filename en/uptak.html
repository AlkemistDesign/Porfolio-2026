CUSTOM THEME DESIGN AND DEVELOPMENT

Uptak: Scalable Design System and Custom Development for Shopify

ROLE

Product Designer & Front-End Developer

TECH STACK

Figma
Shopify Liquid
HTML5/CSS3
JavaScript

AGENCY

BAIANAI

PLATFORM

Web (Shopify eCommerce)

SUMMARY

The initial briefing

Uptak required the launch of its new eCommerce platform on Shopify with a visually powerful and highly customizable design. The starting point was a static visual proposal conceived by an external agency. My role at the BAIANAI agency was to act as the bridge between the visual concept and technical reality.

As a UX/UI designer and Front-End developer, I took on the responsibility of auditing and restructuring the initial proposal in Figma, expanding the experience to responsive screens, designing a system of scalable atomic components and developing the custom theme in Shopify from scratch, ensuring the client's complete autonomy to manage their store.

APPROACH

The Problem and Objectives

The main challenge lay in the technical feasibility and user experience of the initial proposal. The inherited design lacked logic for on-screen viewing, structured responsive behaviors, and consistency among the styles of its elements.

Key objectives:

  • Web Optimization and Adaptation: Translate a static visual proposal to a real web environment in Figma, resolving inconsistencies and adapting the experience to responsive resolutions (Mobile, Tablet, Desktop).
  • Creation of a Design System: Unify styles, typography, colors, and components to ensure visual consistency across the newly developed pages.
  • Autonomy and Scalability in Shopify: Design and program a modular architecture based on configurable blocks in Shopify, allowing the client to intuitively duplicate and edit sections without needing to touch code.

BENCHMARKING

Research

Since the overall aesthetic concept was already predefined, the research phase focused on a competitive analysis (benchmarking) centered on responsive interaction and eCommerce conversion, as the critical pages of the purchase flow had not been designed.
I studied how leading brands in the sector structured their user flows, product detail pages (PDP), and slide-out carts. The goal was to identify usability best practices to apply them when expanding the sitemap and designing the required new pages, ensuring that the brand's bold aesthetic did not interfere with a smooth and accessible user experience.

DESIGN SYSTEM

Design Strategy and Atomic System

To resolve the lack of consistency, I deconstructed the initial design in Figma, transforming it into an atomic design system.

Key points:

  • Style Unification: I defined clear design tokens for colors, spacing, and a strict typographic scale that guaranteed readability.
  • Component Design and Responsive Iteration: I created master components in Figma with dynamic variants to address the responsive behavior of complex elements like product tables, navigation menus, and collection layouts.
  • UI Expansion: I designed the missing secondary pages and user flows, ensuring each new view integrated organically with the approved visual identity.

FRONT-END DEVELOPMENT

Liquid and Modularity in Shopify

The core of the project was translating the Figma design system into native code within a custom Shopify theme (using Liquid, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript).

I precisely defined which properties should remain fixed to maintain brand consistency (like fonts and base alignments) and which should be flexible for the client (texts, background images, links, and button visibility). This way, the end user got a robust, fully editable drag-and-drop interface.

CONCLUSIONS

Results and learning

The project concluded with the successful launch of the Uptak website, strictly meeting the brand's visual standards and the business's self-management needs.

Main Learnings and Conclusions:

  • Development-Oriented Design: I consolidated my ability to create clean Figma files with precise auto-layouts and comprehensive technical documentation, reducing doubts to zero during the layout phase.
  • Scalable Design Systems: I learned the value of structuring systems thinking not only about the current state of the product but also about how users without technical knowledge will interact and expand the platform in the long term.
  • End-to-End Product Vision: This case demonstrates my ability to lead projects from the conceptual UI phase to the delivery of final code in production, ensuring not a single ounce of design quality is lost in the development process.
